**Global Net Fund (GNF) – Saving Group Support**

GNF Saving Group Support is a dedicated digital support platform designed to help members of Global Net Fund (GNF) access assistance, manage their savings group activities, track contributions, and receive guidance on their accounts and transactions.

The platform provides a centralized support experience for members, offering assistance with account registration, wallet access, USDC payments, savings contributions, transaction records, account security, and general enquiries.

Built around transparency, security, accessibility, and financial cooperation, GNF aims to make group saving more organized and accessible for members worldwide.

**Our Mission:** To simplify collective saving, strengthen financial cooperation, and support members as they work towards shared financial goals.

**GNF – Save Together. Build Together. Grow Together.**
